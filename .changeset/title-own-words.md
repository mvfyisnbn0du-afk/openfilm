Studio: a title written with its subtitle inside it (`<div>Title<div>subtitle</div></div>`) can be picked in the picture, and only its own words are picked. Its box hugs its words and the inspector shows them; a double-click types them in place while the subtitle stays shown. Moved, scaled, turned or restyled, the title leaves its subtitle where it was and as it looked: the subtitle's own override that keeps it there is written with the title's edit, one undo. The subtitle is still a layer of its own.
