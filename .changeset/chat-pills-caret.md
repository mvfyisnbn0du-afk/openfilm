Desktop: the caret moves freely around references in the message box. The arrow keys step over a reference in one press instead of stopping on it, where typing did nothing. A click between two references puts the caret there, and a click on one goes where it points with the caret left where it was, no longer thrown to the start of the message. References have a little more room around them.
