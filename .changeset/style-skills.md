Nine more official skills, each with a version, so you can tell which one made a film:
- Workflows: `long-to-short` (a long video cut into shorts that stand alone), `screen-demo` (a screen recording made into a polished demo), `motion-graphics` (studio-level motion graphics), `presenter-pip` (you speaking from a small window while the content fills the screen).
- Styles: `whiteboard` (drawn by hand as a voice explains), `vintage-film` (photos as old film and instant prints), `lyric-video` (a song's lyrics as the picture, every word on the voice), `keynote` (a launch film with your real product on stage), `collage` (cut-paper motion graphics).

A skill's openfilm.json can now give its `version`, its `kind` (`workflow` or `style`), its `order`, an `icon`, a `cover` picture and a `preview` video.

The Community's skills are redesigned: a row of filters at the top (all, yours, this project's, workflows, styles) with adding a skill beside it; yours come first; a note on what skills are for; and a skill opens as a page with its picture or preview on the left and what it is, its version and using it on the right.
