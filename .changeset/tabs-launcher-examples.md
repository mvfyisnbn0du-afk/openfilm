Tabs: the desktop window keeps each open project in a tab of its own, in Studio's top bar; the tab in front stands out as a card, each starts with its film's poster. `+` (⌘T) opens the projects window, and the project picked opens in a new tab (or comes forward, when it is open already). ⌘W closes a tab, ⇧⌘T opens the last one closed again, ⌘1–9 and ⌃Tab switch. A tab shows when an agent is working in it, when one is waiting for your answer, and an export's progress; agents and exports go on in tabs behind. The tabs come back as you left them; with none open, the window shows your projects. The projects window has two views: your projects, and Examples, with a sidebar (Discover, the kinds of film, the skills: official, yours, this project's) where example films play their preview under the pointer and open on a page of their own with their parts, to start a new film from; skills show their own title and summary in your language (a skill's optional `openfilm.json`). An examples index can list each example's `category`, `segments` and a `video` preview; one read from a file has its pictures served by Studio. Studio in a browser keeps its projects window, now with the same two views.
