Tabs: the desktop window keeps each open project in a tab of its own, in Studio's top bar. `+` (⌘T) opens a new tab with the launcher; ⌘W closes a tab, ⇧⌘T opens the last one closed again, ⌘1–9 and ⌃Tab switch. A tab shows when an agent is working in it, when one is waiting for your answer, and an export's progress; agents and exports go on in tabs behind. A project is in one tab at most, and the tabs come back as you left them. The launcher has two views: your projects, and Examples, where example films and skills are grouped by the kind of film they make, play their preview under the pointer, and open on a page of their own with their parts, to start a new film from. An examples index can list each example's `category`, `segments` and a `video` preview; one read from a file has its pictures served by Studio. Studio in a browser keeps its projects window, now with the same two views.
