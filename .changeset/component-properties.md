Page clips have component properties. Select a layer and click ◇ beside its words, its picture or its appearance to make it a property; the clip's Properties section lists only those, each renamed with a double-click, filled in for this clip, and reset on its own or all at once. Variables of the page can be added from the section's +. A clip added to Saved keeps its properties and shows as a component, and every clip taken from it offers them. The Template section that listed everything a page holds is gone.
