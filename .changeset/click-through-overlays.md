Studio: a click on the picture takes what is under a full-frame overlay. When the clip on top has nothing there but a layer filling its page (a letterbox, a vignette, grain laid over the film), the click, the hover outline and a double-click to type go to the title or picture under it in the clip below. Before, the overlay's clip was taken and nothing under it could be picked in the picture.
