Claude Code and Codex sign in to OpenFilm on their own. The first time, OpenFilm asks you to sign in (your browser opens, as in the agent itself); your own sign-in, in your terminal and in the Claude and ChatGPT apps, is never used or touched. Settings → Agents shows whose account each one uses (organization, email and plan for Claude Code; ChatGPT or an API key for Codex). Disconnect signs OpenFilm out, so you can sign in with another account, and Switch account does both in one step.
