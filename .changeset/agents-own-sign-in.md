Claude Code signs in to OpenFilm on its own. The first time, OpenFilm asks you to sign in (your browser opens, as in Claude Code itself); your own sign-in, in your terminal and in the Claude app, is never used or touched. Disconnect signs OpenFilm out, so you can sign in with another account, and Switch account does both in one step. Codex keeps using the account signed in on your computer: switch accounts in Codex, then disconnect and connect it again in OpenFilm. Settings → Agents shows whose account each uses (organization, email and plan).
