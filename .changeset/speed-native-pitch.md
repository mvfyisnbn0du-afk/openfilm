Sound played faster or slower is heard the moment the speed changes, and sounds natural. The browser plays it at that speed with its pitch kept, in the same mix, kept in step with the picture; nothing is made on the server first. 0.2.3 re-encoded the whole sound for each speed, which left long videos silent for seconds after a change and made voices sound hollow.

The viewer's playback button shows a speed gauge instead of the inspector's icon.
