Skills: ways of making a kind of film, each a folder with a SKILL.md and the files it brings. `openfilm skills` lists the skills a film can be made with: the project's own (in its `skills/` folder), yours (in `~/Movies/OpenFilm/Skills`), and the official ones. `openfilm skill <name>` prints one for the agent to follow. `openfilm skill add <GitHub address or folder>` adds a skill to yours and lists any scripts it brings. The manual tells agents about them. Studio no longer lists pages inside a project's `skills/` folder.
