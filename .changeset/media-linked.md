Studio: media kept elsewhere can stay there. Upload offers "Import files…" (a copy in the project, cloned where the disk can: at once, and no room taken), "Link files…" and "Link a folder…" (a link in assets/ to where the media is kept, a NAS or another disk). In the app, a file dropped from Finder is copied on the computer rather than sent through the page. A linked file or folder is marked; one that cannot be reached (the NAS is off) says "Not found". Its menu has "Show original", "Relink…" and "Copy into project"; deleting a link never touches the original. Studio follows a link out of a project only to a place the person linked on this computer, so a project from elsewhere cannot reach their other files; the preview, `openfilm look` and `openfilm render` read linked media, and nothing is written there.
