Studio: the picture in the viewer zooms as Figma's canvas does. Pinch on a trackpad, or hold ⌘ (Ctrl on Windows) and turn the mouse wheel, to zoom about the pointer; while it is zoomed, scroll (Shift + wheel: sideways) or drag with the middle button to pan. The zoom shows in the viewer's bar, and pressing it fits the picture again. Selecting, moving and resizing on the picture work at any zoom, with handles the same size on screen. The zoom changes only the viewer, never the film or its exports.
