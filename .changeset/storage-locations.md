Choose where your work is kept. Settings → General → Storage shows where new projects and exports go, with the free space on each disk, and lets you change either folder (or go back to the default), for example to keep large projects off a full system disk. New projects, including ones fetched with `openfilm open`, go to the folder you choose; projects made before stay where they are and still open from the list. On Windows, the installer now asks where to install the app.
