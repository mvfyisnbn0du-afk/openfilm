Choose where new projects go. Settings → General → Storage shows the folder, with the free space on its disk, and lets you change it (or go back to the default), for example to keep large projects off a full system disk. Exports go where you pick when exporting, as before. New projects, including ones fetched with `openfilm open`, go to the folder you choose; projects made before stay where they are and still open from the list. On Windows, the installer now asks where to install the app.
