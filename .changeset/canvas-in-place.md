Studio: a canvas change is applied to the picture in place, without loading the film again, so the film no longer jumps to the top left before it settles in the middle. Dragging the canvas's width or height in the inspector changes the picture as the mark moves. The picture no longer shows a light hairline along its right and bottom edges in the dark theme.
Studio's address keeps its key (`?key=…`), so an address copied from the address bar opens Studio in any browser on this computer, or in an agent's preview, without being signed out. The picture is clipped to its own box, so no line shows along its edges. The inspector is open whenever a project opens. A canvas change fits each picture to the new canvas on its own, as CapCut does, so a portrait photo on one clip no longer shrinks 16:9 footage on another. Dragging the canvas's width or height shows the size and shape on the picture and in the inspector as it moves (Custom unless it is a preset). A click on a page's empty part selects the whole page, so a page smaller than the canvas can be moved and sized on the picture. A canvas side typed or dragged stays from 16 to 8192 px and within 1:4 to 4:1. With a whole page in hand, a click on something in it selects that (a drag still moves the page). ⌘A (Ctrl+A) selects every layer on the picture; Shift-click and a drag on empty space select several, as before.
