Studio: with the main-track magnet on, clips on the other tracks never cover each other, as in CapCut. A clip dragged, pasted, duplicated or dropped from the media pane where another one already is goes to the nearest free track of its kind, or a new one opens for it, instead of cutting what was there. Nudging with ⌥← / ⌥→ stops at a neighbor. Hold Alt while dropping to overwrite anyway.
