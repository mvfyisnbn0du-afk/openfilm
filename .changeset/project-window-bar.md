Studio: the Projects list says what belongs to what, and a new window is made from under the project it goes in. The list used to draw a window beside the project, in a grid of its own, so a window read as a project of its own and nothing said which project it was in; a window is now drawn **under** the project it is in, as that project's own, and the project has a bar right under its card where another window in it is made. A window of a window is one step further in, and the windows of a project that is not in the list are not lost: they stand on their own.
