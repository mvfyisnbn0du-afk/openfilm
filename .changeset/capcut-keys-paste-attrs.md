Studio: CapCut's keys (its Mac set) and its paste attributes. ⌘⇧C copies a clip's attributes and ⌘⇧V pastes them onto the selected clips, with CapCut's dialog to pick position and size, blending, volume and fades (also in the clip's menu). P turns the main-track magnet on or off, ~ linkage, ⇧X sets in and out to the selected clips, ⌥X clears them, ⌘R opens the clip's speed, ⇧⌃S detaches a video's sound, ⌥⇧M goes to the previous marker, and ⇧← / ⇧→ move the playhead 10 frames.
