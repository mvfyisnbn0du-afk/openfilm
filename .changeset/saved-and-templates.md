Saved: keep a clip, several clips together, or a file to use again in any project. Right-click a clip on the timeline or the picture, or a file in the media pane, and choose Add to Saved; with several clips selected, save them as one (they come back on tracks of their own, laid out as they were, links kept) or each alone. The bookmark button beside Upload switches the media pane to what you saved, with a picture of each; drag one onto the timeline, or double-click it to put it in at the playhead. A clip comes back as you saved it, with its place, its look and the changes made in its page, and the files it needs are copied into the project, so changing it there never changes the saved one. Saved things are kept in a Saved folder beside your projects; rename one, show it in its folder, or move it to the Trash from its menu. The media pane no longer has a close button: the assets button beside the chat opens and closes it.

Template: select a page clip and the inspector shows what its page lets you change, without opening the page: each of its words, each of its pictures (replace one with a picture of the project, one dropped from the media pane, or one from your computer), and the CSS custom properties its stylesheet sets on `:root` (a color with a color picker, a number with its unit, a font with the font picker, words in quotes as text). Each change is kept for that clip only, and one changed can go back to the page's own. A page saved with such properties is marked as a template in Saved.
