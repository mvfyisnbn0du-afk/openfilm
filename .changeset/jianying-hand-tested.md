Studio: four more things work as in CapCut (剪映, tried by hand). With the split tool, ⇧-click cuts every track at that point. ⌥-drag copies a clip (or the selection it is in): the clip stays and a copy lands where you let go, inserted on the main track and on a free track elsewhere. Clips selected together and dragged above or below every track open new tracks there, one for each track they came from. With linkage on, deleting a main-track clip deletes the titles, B-roll and sound riding on it; a music bed under several shots stays. Slip and slide are no longer ⌥-drag gestures; the inspector's source bar still slips a clip.
