A `text` override keeps line breaks: on an element of words alone, or of lines between `<br>`s, it replaces all of the words, a `<br>` at each line break (SPEC §1). In Studio, a double-click on such a line types in it in place, its lines and width kept; Shift+Enter breaks a line, Enter keeps the words, Esc gives them up.
