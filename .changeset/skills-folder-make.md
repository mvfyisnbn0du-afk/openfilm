Skills: the Skills button adds a skill from a link or from a folder. Choose the folder, or drop it, or its SKILL.md, on the list; you see what it brings before it is copied into yours. "Make one with the agent" puts a message in the composer asking the agent to write what was done into a skill of yours. The button also opens your skills folder and a form for feedback on skills.
