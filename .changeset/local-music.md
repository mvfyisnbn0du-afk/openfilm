Music and sound effects from a local model: the new `local` provider runs a command the person points at
(Settings → Providers → Local model, or `OPENFILM_LOCAL_MUSIC_CMD`) for `openfilm get music/sfx --via local`.
Whatever audio file it leaves behind is the take; nothing is billed and no key is needed. Recipes for MusicGen
Small and other local models in docs/local-music.md.
