Studio: the export dialog says which media files are missing before anything is queued, and holds back exports that draw or play the film until they are relinked (a project, subtitles or an editing project still export). The timeline's toolbar is simpler: three switches in CapCut's order, main-track magnet, snapping and linkage, each with a one-line tip. Sync lock is gone (linkage does its job), and a video and its detached sound are always selected together (⌥-click picks one).
