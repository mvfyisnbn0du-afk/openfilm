Studio: a clip's inspector has a Replace button: another file of the project in its place, or one picked from your computer (also in the clip's menu, under Replace with…). The media pane lists files in the order they were added, the newest last, and selects what you just imported. In the viewer, a click in the dark around the picture no longer pauses the film: it stops only once a selection box is pulled open there.
