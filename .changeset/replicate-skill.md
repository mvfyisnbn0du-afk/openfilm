An official skill, `replicate`: give the agent a video and it makes yours like it, keeping its structure, rhythm, look and motion while the content is yours. It watches the reference whole and in stretches with `openfilm look`, starts from an example's source when one fits, writes a plan part by part, and compares the two side by side. It never reuses the reference's footage, music, voice or words.
