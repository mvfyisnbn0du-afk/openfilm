Studio: restyling a layer no longer moves the others. A page often lines words up in a row (a number and the words beside it); a wider or larger font for the number pushed the words along. Now the layer takes its new size (its selection box grows with a larger font) while margins give the page back the room it gained, written with its style, so the words beside it, under it or lined up with its baseline stay where they are, while the menu previews a font too and when its line height changes. A width or height you set yourself still moves what is around it.
