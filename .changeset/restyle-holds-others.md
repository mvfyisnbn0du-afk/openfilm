Studio: restyling a layer no longer moves the others, as in Figma. A page often lines words up in a row (a number and the words beside it); a wider or larger font for the number pushed the words along. Now the layer keeps the place it had in the page, written with its style, and the words beside it stay where they are, while the menu previews a font too. A width or height you set yourself still moves what is around it.
