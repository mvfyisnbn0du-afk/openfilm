Studio: a still or video dropped on the timeline keeps its own shape, as large as the canvas holds and centered, so its box is the picture and needs no crop. A phone's portrait video is read at the size it is shown.
The media pane takes any kind of file (a PDF, a document, a model): what Studio cannot play shows with its icon and ending, under a new "Other" filter. Several files and folders can be picked at once (⌘-click, ⇧-click, a box dragged over empty space, ⌘A) and moved or deleted together; every file and folder has "Move to" on its right-click menu. In a narrow pane the top bar folds Upload and then the search into icons instead of crowding.
