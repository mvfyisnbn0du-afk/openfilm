Studio: a still or video dropped on the timeline keeps its own shape, as large as the canvas holds and centered, so its box is the picture and needs no crop. A phone's portrait video is read at the size it is shown.
The media pane takes any kind of file (a PDF, a document, a model): what Studio cannot play shows with its icon and ending, under a new "Other" filter. Several files and folders can be picked at once (⌘-click, ⇧-click, a box dragged over empty space, ⌘A) and moved or deleted together; every file and folder has "Move to" on its right-click menu. 
A web page that sets no size of its own keeps the size it shows alone (1920 × 1080) when dropped, scaled whole into the canvas, instead of being laid out again in the canvas's shape. A page put in a media folder keeps its poster and plays as a page. The list view shows each file's type, dimensions, size and length as the pane widens; the tiles lose their "+" (drag a file to the timeline, or press Enter); the filter and sort menus close when their button is clicked again. The top bar fills its width: narrowing, Upload becomes its icon first, then the search field shortens, and only at the narrowest is the search a button that opens over the bar.
An import shows a card for each file where it goes, its own progress inside (a picture shows itself while it is sent), in place of the bar above the media; a folder it goes into shows the progress on its cover.
