Projects and tabs keep in step. A project deleted (in the Projects window, another tab, by an agent) or whose folder is gone closes its tabs without asking, all of them together, and the front goes to a tab that stays; none of them comes back with Reopen Closed Tab. The Projects window never stays on "Creating project" or "Opening project", reads the list again whenever it is shown, and has the tabs in its top bar while any are open. Closing the last tab starts the next launch on the Projects window. Changes to the projects list made at the same moment (several deleted together, one made meanwhile) all hold, and a rename shows in its tab's title.
