In the chat, a skill can be named in a message: type `/`, or pick one in the Community; it goes in as a pill and the agent gets the skill's instructions with the message. The composer's Community button opens over your film on the skills (official, yours, this project's), with the examples beside them: point the agent at an example, or a part of one (a segment, or a stretch dragged on its strip), and it reads how that film is made; or start a new film from it, in a tab of its own. Add a skill there from a GitHub link or a folder, or make one with the agent. In OpenFilm an agent keeps its maker's own skills and gets OpenFilm's, but the skills you installed in it yourself stay off until you turn each on in Settings → Agents. This works for Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode and your own key's agent; agents that can't be told say they use all of yours. The context ring is gone: when a conversation is nearly full, a line above the composer offers a new one.
