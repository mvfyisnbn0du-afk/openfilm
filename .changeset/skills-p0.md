Your own skills are kept in `~/Movies/OpenFilm/Skills` (beside Projects and Exports, backed up with them); skills kept in `~/.openfilm/skills` move there on their own. A skill replaced or removed goes to the trash, never deleted, and `openfilm skill remove <name>` moves one there. Adding from a link or a folder shows what came first: each skill, its size, the scripts it brings and any of yours it would replace, then Add or Cancel. The switches for an agent's own skills are in Settings → Agents, beside its MCP servers. OpenFilm's own skill for terminal agents is no longer shown in the app.
