Desktop: the ruler points at time for the chat. A click on it puts that moment in the message, a drag that span: the moment under the pointer is drawn as a line with a tag of its time and the chat's mark, a drag as its span; the playhead stays where it is (its knob drags it, a click in the tracks' empty space moves it). A clip or a subtitle line referred to the chat no longer shows its time on its pill (it is on its card): a pill shows time only when it is time.
