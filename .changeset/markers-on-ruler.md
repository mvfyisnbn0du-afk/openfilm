Studio: markers are simpler. Add one with M, or right-click the ruler and pick "Add marker". Each marker is a small pin on the ruler: hover it for its name and time, click it to go there and open its card (name, color, delete), drag it to move it. M on a marker that is already there opens its card ready to name it. The markers button and list in the toolbar are gone. A click on a pin under the playhead opens its card too, and a name typed in the card is kept when the card closes.
