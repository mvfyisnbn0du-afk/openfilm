Studio: markers are simpler. Add one with the marker button left of split, with M, or by right-clicking the ruler. Markers sit on their own row under the ruler (under the subtitle row when it shows), each a small tab pointing down at its moment with its name beside it. Hover one for its time and a dashed line through the whole timeline; drag it to move it, snapping to clip edges and the playhead with that line shown; click it to go there and open its card (name, color, delete). A click or drag on the row between markers moves the playhead, as in the tracks' empty space. M on a marker that is already there opens its card ready to name it. The old markers list in the toolbar is gone, and a name typed in the card is kept when the card closes.
