# Collage

## The standard

Collage that looks made by a skilled hand and an art director: real paper texture and light, cut-outs with clean or
torn edges and soft shadows, print grain, a palette chosen for the subject, every composition balanced. Not stickers
dropped on a flat background.

## The bar

- **Real material**: paper fibers, tears, folds, tape, halftone dots, misregistered print, scanned-looking marks;
  shadows and light that make layers sit on each other.
- **Cut-outs that serve the story**: the person's photos or found, generated or drawn pictures, cut and combined so
  that they say something; heads, objects and words collaged with wit.
- **Stop-motion life**: pieces move on twos or threes with a little jitter, slide, flip, tear and stick; the camera
  can move over the table.
- **Type**: cut-out letters, labels, stamps and handwriting that stay readable.
- **One direction** written down first (`direction.md`), and the whole film judged at full size against the best
  collage work you know, then refined.

## Freedom

Use anything that makes it: CSS and SVG masks for cut edges, canvas or shaders for paper and print, real textures,
background removal for cut-outs, generated pictures when a service is connected, Blender or any renderer whose output
becomes a clip. The rules are only OpenFilm's: the picture at t depends on t alone, and a page has no sound.
